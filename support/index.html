Support

Have a question, found a bug, or have an idea for EODTOC? Get in touch at dev@eodtoc.com.

Can we recover your encrypted records?

The developer cannot access your stored Ops or AARs and never has access to your private decryption key. Keep your recovery key somewhere secure off your device.

Why are intelligence features unavailable?

These optional features require a device that supports Apple Intelligence, with Apple Intelligence enabled. Availability also depends on Apple's language and region support.

Bug Reports

Include your EODTOC version, device and OS (e.g. iPhone 17, iOS 27.1), and what went wrong. Screenshots can be helpful, but they must not include any sensitive information. EODTOC developers will never ask you for any personal or sensitive data.
